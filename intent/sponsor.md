Feature: Sponsor Page

Intent

Create a sponsor-facing page for Intent Driven Saturdays that makes the opportunity understandable and commercially credible within a few minutes.

The page should answer one question:

Why should my company sponsor one of these challenges?

Do not turn this into a long corporate sales site. Keep it sharp, credible, visual, and easy to act on.

Desired Outcome

A potential employer, recruiter, technology company, or corporate innovation leader should be able to visit the page and quickly understand:

what Intent Driven Saturdays is

what the sponsor funds

what participants do

what the sponsor receives

why the model is different from a traditional hackathon

approximate sponsorship cost

how to start a conversation

The page should make the concept feel real enough to sponsor now, even though the event platform will continue evolving.

Core Brand

Intent Driven Saturdays

Build Saturday. Win Money. Bring the Learning Back to Your Corporation on Monday.

Supporting positioning:

Stop screening claims. Start evaluating evidence.

25 Candidates. 4 Hours. One Challenge.

Hire from evidence, not résumés.

The emphasis is not simply on writing code.

Participants create evidence of:

problem solving

reasoning

architecture

implementation

collaboration

validation

creativity

delivery under equal constraints

How It Works

Communicate a simple event model:

1. Sponsor the Challenge

A sponsor funds the event, prize pool, or both.

The sponsor may provide:

a synthetic business problem

a sanitized representative problem

a technology-focused challenge

a neutral challenge created by Intent Driven Saturdays

Do not imply that participants perform unpaid production work for the sponsor.

2. Builders Compete

Approximately 25 participants compete individually or in small teams under the same:

challenge

time limit

rules

starting conditions

Typical challenge duration:

4 hours

3. Evidence Is Produced

Teams produce working evidence such as:

running solution

repository

reasoning

design decisions

validation

demonstration

lessons learned

4. Sponsor Sees the Results

Sponsors can observe final demonstrations and see which participants or teams produced the strongest evidence.

The sponsor may discover:

potential hires

internal talent

new technical approaches

innovative ideas

solutions the organization had not considered

Why Sponsor

Make the value proposition prominent.

Traditional recruiting asks:

What does this candidate claim they can do?

Intent Driven Saturdays asks:

What can they actually demonstrate?

Traditional innovation programs may spend weeks discussing ideas.

Intent Driven Saturdays creates evidence in hours.

technology adoption

internal team development

access to final demonstrations

summarized challenge results
